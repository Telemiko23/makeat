# Makeat

Makeat helps Israeli households save money on groceries. A friendly onboarding turns household size, meals and dietary needs into a varied, editable recipe plan, then compares the full basket across Israeli retail chains with live prices and delivery fees, and guides the cook step by step.

Hebrew first, with Arabic, Russian and English to follow. Android and iOS.
